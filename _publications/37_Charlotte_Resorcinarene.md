excerpt: 'Resorcinarene capsules constitute a promising class of self-assembled supramolecular hosts, able to catalyse reactions characterized by cationic transition states with remarkable regio-, size- and stereoselectivities, as shown by recent examples. However, the characterization of their structure, speciation and interactions with encapsulated guests remains an outstanding analytical challenge. Here, we examine the encapsulation of transition metal (TM) complexes into resorcinarene capsules by ESI-MS, ion mobility spectrometry (IMS), tandem MS and hydrogen-deuterium exchange (HDX) experiments. Integrating results from these complementary reveals that the identification and characterization of self-assembled supramolecular capsules is far from trivial, as the detected capsules are in fact either fragment ions from specific hexameric capsules, or non-specific artifacts produced in the ESI source of our instrument.'
